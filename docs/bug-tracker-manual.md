# Relaxed-play capture guide

This is a normal-play session, not a development task. Play as usual and send
Codex any bug or integration thought in plain language. No form or investigation
is required while you play. I will record bug reports in the Project Zomboid
normal-play tracker and keep feature ideas in [feature-ideas.md](feature-ideas.md).

## What to send

A short message is enough: “The RV heater says it is on but the cabin is still
cold,” “the inventory froze just now,” or “It recovered after I closed the map.”
If convenient, include what you expected, what happened, roughly when it began,
what you were doing, where you were, and whether it recovered. Unknown details
stay unknown. You do not need to stop and reproduce anything or open the console.

For a follow-up, mention the issue ID I give you or describe the same symptom.
I will attach a recurrence to its existing issue when it is clearly the same
problem; a separate symptom gets its own entry. I will preserve your wording and
record report time separately from when you think it began.
